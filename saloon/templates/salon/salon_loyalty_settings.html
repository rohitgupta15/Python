📖 How It Works

Earning Points

Customers earn loyalty points automatically when they make a purchase. The points are calculated based on the total bill amount.

Redeeming Points

During checkout, customers can redeem their accumulated points for a discount on their bill. The discount value is calculated by multiplying points by the redemption value.

Example

  • Customer spends ₹500
  • With 1 point per ₹1 = 500 points earned
  • Next visit, customer redeems 500 points
  • Discount = 500 × ₹0.25 = ₹125 discount!
